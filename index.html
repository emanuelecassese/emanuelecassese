About

I’ve spent the last five years turning designs into products people use every day, from a booking platform with 40,000 monthly users to small tools for local businesses. I care about the details nobody notices until they’re missing: loading states, keyboard navigation, the half-second that makes a page feel fast.

Outside of work I make pixel art and tiny web experiments. Drawing on a 32×32 grid taught me more about design than any course: every pixel has to earn its place.
